# Art Direction

## System-facing rules

- Alebrije patterns must support stun buildup and approximate stun decay readability.
- Player body patterns expand permanently with progression and change emission by ritual availability/cooldown.
- Restoration should create readable but not necessarily instant world beautification.
- Corruption must remain visually distinguishable from physical injury.
