# Audio Design Direction

Akbal treats music as gameplay structure and world-state feedback.

## Established rules

- Bosses have genre identities expressed by composition, movement and attacks.
- One encounter keeps one composition identity while arrangement/layers may vary.
- Normal-zone music is rebuilt from restored resident alebrijes.
- Boss-zone music is separate but shares motifs with its normal zone.
- Outer-zone shortcuts blend both connected genres.
- Routes from each outer zone to the center use a broken form of that outer genre.
- The Fragmented center combines/breaks all three outer identities.
