# Instruments, Channels and Gating

## Instruments

| Order | Instrument | Channel archetype |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Teponaztli | Percussion |
| 2 | Tololoche | Low Pitch |
| 3 | Cuatro | Medium Pitch |
| 4 | TBD multi-tonal instrument | High Register |

The fourth instrument's stable technical identifier is `HighRegister` until art/narrative selection is final.

## Instrument meaning

An instrument unlocks a family of playable channels, not a single sound asset.

## Metroidvania gating

Alebrijes, routes, secrets, power-ups, and optional interactions may require a channel archetype. Missing instruments should be readable as a capability gap rather than a failed input.

## Progression

Major/significant bonds grant instruments or major capabilities. Ordinary alebrijes primarily contribute world/music restoration, knowledge, access, or lesser rewards.
