# Ritual and Synchronization

## Sync bar

The alebrije has one segmented Synchronization bar.

## Success

Successful required inputs increase Sync according to chart/tuning. Completing a section creates a solidified threshold.

## Passive decay

If ritual stops with temporary progress above the latest solidified anchor, that temporary progress drains to the anchor.

## Error regression

Ritual mistakes actively reduce Sync and can break completed/Soothing anchors.

When errors accumulate in Section N:

1. Current Section N can drain fully.
2. Previous Section N-1 can then be desolidified and drain.
3. Regression floor is the midpoint of Section N-1.
4. Reaching the configured failure floor can force ritual exit.

For Section 1, the minimum floor is the beginning of the Sync bar.

## Escalation

Synchronization also drives encounter escalation through separately authored curves for alebrije pressure and ritual complexity.

## Soothing

Soothing creates a solidified anchor at the exact current Sync value, then immediately ends ritual, removes stun, and starts cooldown. The anchor protects from passive decay, not future ritual mistakes.
