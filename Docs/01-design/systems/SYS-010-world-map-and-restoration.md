# World Map and Restoration

## Topology

Three outer zones A/B/C form a triangle. Each outer zone connects to both other outer zones and to the central Fragmented zone.

## Outer shortcuts

A-B, A-C, and B-C shortcuts use authored musical combinations of both connected genres.

## Center approaches

A-Center, B-Center, C-Center use broken/corrupted versions of the originating outer zone genre rather than a simple genre blend.

## Metroidvania return paths

Nodes may contain routes, encounters, treasures, or power-ups gated by future instruments. The map should support meaningful return visits rather than one-way linear progression.

## Persistent restoration

Normal alebrijes cause subtle local changes. Boss restoration causes unique persistent boss-zone transformations and activates that arena as a checkpoint.
