# Abilities, Power-ups and Specialization

## Ritual active abilities

Fixed set; no ritual loadout selection.

1. **Grace** — limited mistake tolerance.
2. **Resonance Window** — temporarily expands timing acceptance.
3. **Echo Assist** — automatically performs the active chart briefly.
4. **Path Ability** — Berserk or Soothing selected at game start.

## Path choice

### Berserk

Boss-only start activation. Push/stun boss. Perfect chain maintains stun indefinitely. Any non-Perfect input ends Berserk, increases corruption, and immediately recovers the boss. Pause is disabled for entire boss encounters on this path.

### Soothing

Usable during ritual. Solidifies current Sync, ends ritual, removes stun, starts cooldown, and causes no extra penalty. Its anchor can be broken by future ritual errors.

Candidates include:

- Dash invincibility frames.
- Defensive posture reduction, capped below 100%.

- Maximum Health.
- Shamanic Charge capacity.
- Shamanic Charge note gain (`BaseNoteCharge`).
- Active heal efficiency.

Lower tiers may be broadly developed; high-tier specializations may exclude one another. Exact tree structure remains `TBD`.
