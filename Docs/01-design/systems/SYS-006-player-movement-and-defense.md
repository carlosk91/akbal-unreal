# Player Movement and Defense

## Feel target

Bullet-hell spatial pressure with hack-and-slash responsiveness and movement, without conventional offensive damage.

## Baseline verbs

- Move.
- Dash.
- Defensive posture.
- Position/use environment.
- Contextual Resonant Reflection against compatible attacks.
- Non-ritual active abilities.
- Ritual enter/exit.

## Dash

Dash is primarily survival/repositioning. Power-ups may increase invincibility-frame duration within balance limits.

## Defensive posture

Reduces eligible incoming damage by a configurable percentage. Reduction must never reach 100%.

## Resonant Reflection

A contextual defensive mechanic for compatible rhythmic threats. It must not become a universal parry requirement. Its exact contribution to stun remains content/system tuning.

## Prohibition

No melee combo, weapon strike, projectile, or ability may reduce an alebrije health pool; alebrijes do not have a combat health pool.
