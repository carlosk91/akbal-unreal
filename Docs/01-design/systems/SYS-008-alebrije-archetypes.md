# Alebrije Archetypes

## Pulse

Stable/cyclic rhythm, gradual drift, repetition, pressure through readable pulse.

## Fractured

One identity whose rhythm has broken: irregular timing, silence, discontinuity, abrupt changes.

## Resonant

Linked creatures/layers; actions affect one another; polyrhythm and shared consequences.

## Fragmented

Composite identity assembled from multiple learned rhythmic/gameplay identities. Strongly associated with the central Fragmented zone.

## Content rule

Archetype describes behavior/rhythmic problem. It is independent from instrument/channel archetype and encounter difficulty.
