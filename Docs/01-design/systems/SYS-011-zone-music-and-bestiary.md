# Zone Music and Bestiary

## Normal-zone music

An unrestored normal zone starts primarily with environmental ambience rather than its complete music theme.

Restoring resident alebrijes adds their authored musical channels/stems to the zone arrangement. Alebrijes gated by future instruments leave corresponding musical material absent until restored.

## Bestiary mixer

For each zone, the bestiary should allow the player to:

- Inspect discovered/restored alebrijes.
- Hear an alebrije's musical contribution.
- Mute/unmute contributions.
- Hear the combined zone theme.
- Understand missing/restored contributions without exposing implementation jargon.

## Boss-zone relationship

Boss music is separate from normal-zone stem restoration but shares motifs, genre vocabulary, and thematic material. Restoring a boss restores the boss-zone music/state only.
