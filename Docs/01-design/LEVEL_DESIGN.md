# Level and World Design Direction

## Topology

Three outer zones form a triangle; every outer zone connects to both others and to the central Fragmented zone.

## Metroidvania rules

- Return paths should reveal meaningful instrument-gated encounters, routes, treasures or power-ups.
- Each boss has a checkpoint before its arena.
- A restored boss arena becomes a checkpoint.
- Normal alebrije restoration causes subtle local changes.
- Boss restoration causes unique boss-zone changes.

## Encounter spaces

Spaces must support bullet-hell readability, responsive close-range movement and ritual risk. Avoid arenas where ritual is trivially safe behind permanent geometry unless intentionally authored.
