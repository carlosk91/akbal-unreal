# Narrative and Cultural Direction

## World identity

Akbal is **original fantasy borrowing selected motifs**, not a claim of strict Maya/Mesoamerican historical reconstruction. The world can contain modern or non-historical musical genres such as techno.

## Known narrative frame

- The protagonist grows through communion/restoration.
- Alebrijes are not disposable enemies.
- The path choice contrasts communion/soothing with domination/Berserk.
- Ending labels in implementation should remain neutral (`Communion`, `Dominion`, `InternalCorruption`) rather than hard-coded moral UI labels.

## Cultural-reference rule

When a real-world instrument, language element, symbol or tradition is used, document whether it is:

1. Directly referenced/researched.
2. Adapted/fantastical.
3. Purely inspired by a broad aesthetic.

Do not imply historical authenticity where the design is intentionally syncretic/original.
